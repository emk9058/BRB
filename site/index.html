BRB

Run League from your phone. The app stays on your PC. The phone joins over your home Wi-Fi.

Download for Windows
  1. Run the installer and open BRB.
  2. If Windows asks, allow BRB on private networks.
  3. Scan the code with a phone on the same Wi-Fi.

BRB is not made by Riot. It controls the League client already open on your computer. Anyone on that Wi-Fi can open the page while BRB is running.
